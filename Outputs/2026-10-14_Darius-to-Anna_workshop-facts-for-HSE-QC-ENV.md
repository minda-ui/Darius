## 1. Hazards in the workshop

**Machines in use**
| Code | Machine | What it does | Main hazards (from the makers' manuals) |
|---|---|---|---|
| FA2303 | Altendorf F45 sliding-table panel saw (CNC fence) | cuts board | blade contact, kickback, tooth ejection, crushing under the pneumatic hold-down (up to 1000 N), live parts, noise, dust. Blade 300–450 mm at 2000–5000 rpm. Laser line (option) |
| FA2304 | Vitap K2 CNC drilling and routing centre | drills and routes panels | crushing of hands/arms at the infeed, moving heads, stored energy (wait at least 10 min after isolation), noise, dust |
| FA2301 | Hebrock F4 edgebander | glues edging to panels | **hot-melt glue at 110–170 °C** (burns, fire), hot surfaces, cutters, noise, dust; oak/beech dust is a health hazard |
| FA2402 | AES SAF 10,000 dust extractor (11 kW) | central extraction for all three machines | electrical (400 V), fire risk in collected dust |
| FA2306 | ABAC screw compressor with dryer, 15 kW, up to 10 bar | air for all three machines | stored pressure; refrigerant in the dryer |
| FA2401 | Brother label printer | labels | none notable |

Electrical: on the F45 the incoming terminals **stay live with the main switch off**, and the drive inverters need **5 minutes**
to discharge. All electrical work is by a qualified electrician.

**Dust and extraction.** One central extractor (FA2402) serves the saw, the drilling centre and the edgebander. Each
machine needs a minimum air speed at its port (F45 and edgebander: 20–25 m/s). **Airflow has never been measured.**
**Whether the installation was formally commissioned is not on record.** The extractor has had three faults in
three weeks (wiring 26/09; motor failure 30/09, new motor fitted 10/10; the correctly sized motor overload is due to be fitted 17/10). While it was
down, dust built up in machine controls and caused a fault (01/10). There is sawdust inside one saw control cabinet.

**Noise.** Saw, router, edgebander and extractor are all loud machines. **No noise readings exist.** The edgebander
manual requires hearing protection.

**Fumes and finishes.** Hot-melt glue fumes at the edgebander. Cleaning solvents: nitro thinner/spirit for the saw,
release/cleaning sprays on the edgebander. **Whether any lacquering or spraying of finishes is done: not in the KB.**

**Manual handling.** Full-size boards are lifted onto the saw by hand. **No lifting aids or
rules are recorded.**

**Lone working.** **Nothing in the KB.** The workshop is often empty for days, and some work is done by one person.

**Pressure system.** The compressor has a receiver (possibly 500 L at 10 bar). **No written scheme of examination and
no examination are on record.** The service label is blank.

## 2. Rules already followed

- **F45:** monthly safety-device check (E-stops, limit switches, switch, hood), logged on the Safety Check Log; a fail
  means stop work. *The log has no entry yet.* The saw's own inspection certificates expired 22/02/2024.
- **Vitap:** the maker's functional safety test before automatic running. Not logged.
- **Edgebander:** E-stop and air check daily (maker's instruction).
- **Fault rule:** any fault on a guard or E-stop means stop work; never bridge or bypass a safety device.
- **Maintenance:** done switched off and locked off, to a schedule (33 recurring tasks, tracked on Smartsheet).
- **Who may use which machine:** the makers' manuals say "trained and authorised persons only" (edgebander) and set
  operator categories (Vitap). **Who is actually authorised in this workshop is not recorded.**
- **Inductions, PPE rules, first aid, fire points:** **not in the KB.** PPE named in the manuals: hearing protection,
  safety shoes, gloves, close-fitting clothing; CO2 extinguisher for electrical fires.

## 3. Quality checks already done

- **Before production:** every job's drawings and machine programs are checked against each other and the hardware
  makers' data (drill positions, part sizes, wall fixing); nothing goes to the machines with an open fault.
- **Machine faults** that affect quality (chipping, burn marks, out-of-square) are logged with their fix.
- **Checks on finished furniture before it leaves: not in the KB.**

## 4. Waste and environment

- **Sawdust and chips:** collected by the extractor into metal bins. **Where it goes after that, and who collects:
  not in the KB.**
- **Offcuts:** reused where possible; an offcut store is planned, not built. **Disposal route: not in the KB.**
- **Glue, solvents, cleaning sprays, compressor condensate and oil:** **disposal not in the KB.**
- **Refrigerant (compressor dryer):** R513A, 0.5 kg (0.316 t CO2e) — below the 5 t F-Gas leak-check threshold; any work on
  it by certified people only.
- **Permits:** none recorded.

## 5. What does not fit a site-job procedure

- **Fixed machinery, all day, one place:** PUWER inspections and records per machine, guards and interlocks — not a
  site-by-site risk assessment.
- **Wood dust (WEL) and LEV:** the extractor is local exhaust ventilation; the law expects a thorough examination
  every 14 months — none on record.
- **Pressure system (PSSR)** for the compressor.
- **Noise at work** assessment for the workshop as a place.
- **Fire:** hot-melt glue plus fine dust in one room.
- **Quality** is checked on drawings and programs before cutting, not on site after fitting.
- **Waste** is a steady trade-waste stream (dust, offcuts), not job-by-job skips.
