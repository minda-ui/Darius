**Subject:** Corner cabinet ("Angolare", straight front) — Cabineo shelf joints on sides and shelves do not line up (19 mm)

Hello,

We designed a diagonal corner wall unit with the horizontal shape **"Angolare"**, front joining the sides in a straight
line: width 600, both depths 300, height 900, 19 mm board, two shelves. Shelf joints are **Cabineo** (2 per edge, front
distance 50, rear distance 40, in the cabinet's CAM joint configuration).

In the exported CN programs the shelf joints on the **sides** and on the **shelves** do not match:

- The **rear** joint lines up (about 75 mm from the wall on both parts).
- The **front** joint does not: side ≈ **240 mm** from the wall, shelf ≈ **221.5 mm** — a constant **19 mm** (one board
  thickness). The spacing between the two joints is 165 on the side and 146 on the shelf (earlier export: 126 vs 107).

The sides come out **319 mm** deep (300 + 19) while the shelf's side edge follows the 300 line of the top and bottom, so
"50 from the front" is measured from two different fronts. Changing the front/rear distances or the number of joints moves
both parts together, so we cannot correct it in the joint table.

Could you check how the shelf joint positions are calculated for the "Angolare" shape with a straight front, or tell us
which setting makes the sides and shelves use the same reference?

For now we are using plain shelf pins for this unit.

Thank you,
Minda Gaudiesius — AMFA Furniture workshop
