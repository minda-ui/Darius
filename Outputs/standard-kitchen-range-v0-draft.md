## 1. What "standard" means

A standard kitchen is built **only from the units in this catalogue**, at the sizes listed. The customer chooses:

- **carcass colour** (from the standard palette);
- **front colour** (from the standard palette);
- **edging** (matching or contrast, from the standard palette);
- **handles**: **[TBC]** whether handles are a customer choice and from which list, or the range is handle-less.

**Everything else is fixed**: sizes, construction and hardware. That is what lets the workshop give **a clear
delivery term**: **[TBC] N working weeks from release** (release = contract signed + order form complete, see §5).

**Anything outside the catalogue is bespoke** (a different size, a cut-down unit, a special front). It is quoted and
scheduled separately, and it changes the delivery term. **[TBC]** the owner may allow a short list of fixed
modifications (e.g. width cut) with a fixed extra time each.

### Decor end panels — standard sizes (added 2026-10-04)

Same board and edge as the fronts. **Front edge flush with the door face**:
- base: carcass 570 + hinge gap 1.5 + door 19 = 591;
- wall: 300 + 1.5 + 19 = 321.

**Every panel is +25 mm deeper at the back, for the fitter to scribe to the wall.** Base panels are also
+10 mm taller for the floor. **The back edge is not edged**, because it is scribed on site.

| Code | Use | Size H × D (mm) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| `P-END-B` | base, 150 legs | **880 × 616** | 870 + 10 scribe; fitters may ask for 890 |
| `P-END-W90` | wall, 900 high | **900 × 346** | |
| `P-END-W90L` | wall, 900 high, **with LED under the units** | **950 × 346** | +50 drop hides the strip |
| `P-END-W72` | wall, 720 high | **720 × 346** | |
| `P-END-W72L` | wall, 720 high, with LED | **770 × 346** | |
| `P-END-T` | tall | **[TBC]** | tall height not yet decided |

*Benchmarks, read 2026-10-04:*
- **Howdens' manual:** decor ends fitted flush with the fronts and "scribed to the wall as required"; base
  890 = 720 + 170 legs.
- **DIY Kitchens:** wall 772 / 952 × 325 (+52 drop), base 900 × 600.

**Board use (2800 × 2070, plain decor):** 6 × `P-END-W90` + 1 × `P-END-B` in one 900 row, 2 + 3 in the second
row. That is **12 panels per board**. A woodgrain decor must keep its grain vertical, so it fits fewer.

**Appliance housings** fit appliances **to the manufacturer's built-in dimensions**. Sales give the **model number**
on the order form, and the workshop checks it fits before release.

## 5. What Sales send to release a standard kitchen

1. **Sales job reference** (CRM) and **contract signed** (+ deposit, if required [TBC]).
2. **Order form**: one line per unit, giving the **code**, **quantity** and **door hand (L/R)** where it applies.
   Plus the **colour choices** (carcass / fronts / edging / handles).
3. **The 3D render** of the layout and **the room's measured dimensions** (wall lengths, ceiling height), **with pipes,
   sockets and boxing marked**.
4. **Appliance model numbers**, if any housings are ordered.
5. **Access**: anything that limits unit size on delivery (stairs, narrow doors).

The workshop checks the form against the render and dimensions: the units must add up to the wall, with fillers. It
then releases the job with a fixed delivery date. **The workshop never contacts the customer**; questions go back to
Sales.
