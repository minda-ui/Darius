# Change log — 2026-09-15 — Corner-rounding router incident resolved

**Session 5.** Follow-up to Session 4's in-progress incident on the Hebrock F4 (`FA2301`)
corner-rounding station.

## What happened

Owner tried the recommended fix — reversing direction on the stop screw (turning it back **in**
partway from the first, overcorrected adjustment) — and confirmed with a photo: a clean, properly
rounded corner on both edges, no marks, no gouging.

## What was done

- **Wiki article** (`Machinery/hebrock-f4-next-edge-bander.md`) Incidents section updated from
 "in progress" to **RESOLVED**, with the full timeline (first adjustment overcorrected, second
 adjustment fixed it) and a lesson-learned note: the manual's fault-table wording implies backing
 the stop screw *out* increases cutting engagement, but the setting that actually worked was
 reached by going back *in* from the overcorrected position. This is recorded as a discrepancy
 between the documented mechanism and the observed result, not resolved by picking a theory — the
 practical lesson kept is the method (small reversible steps, test cut after each) rather than a
 claimed mechanical explanation.
- Fourth fault photo filed to `Raw/` (`2026-09-15_fault-photo_edge-bander-corner-rounding-fixed.jpg`)
 and cited.
- **Smartsheet**: Task **T006** marked Done with the resolution and lesson-learned note.
 `FA2301`'s Status returned to **"In service"** (from "Under repair"), Note updated to reflect the
 fix.
