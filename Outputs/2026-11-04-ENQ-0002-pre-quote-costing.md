## 4. Lead time
No supplier for the fronts now, so **the workshop sets it**: board and hardware usually next-day, then **1.5–2 working
days** in the workshop → **about 3–4 working days from go-ahead**, if the job goes straight in. The workshop's queue at
the time (Minda) can push that out. Knobs must be in before drilling, or the fronts are drilled last.

## 5. What blocks a firm price
1. ⚠ **Board, edging and Innobox prices are web or indicative**, not a supplier quote (Innobox £17.95–£23.95 ex VAT; at
   the higher figure ≈ +£25 inc VAT). A Lathams / supplier quote fixes this.
2. **Front finish** — 18 mm W1100 ST9 slab is my assumption; another board, a thicker edge or a painted front changes
   the price.
3. **Niche size.** Two 500 units need an opening **≥ 1,006–1,010 mm** wide, ≥ 506 high, ≥ 500 deep — or fillers/
   scribes, which add time. Needs measuring on site.
4. **Knobs** — model, price, who supplies; sets the drill size.
5. **Who fits** — us (+ £90 + travel) or the client.
