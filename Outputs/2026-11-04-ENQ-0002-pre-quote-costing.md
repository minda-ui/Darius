## 5. What blocks a firm price
1. ⚠ **Board and Innobox prices are web or indicative**, not a supplier quote (Innobox seen at £17.95–£23.95 ex VAT;
   at the higher figure materials rise by £18 and the total by ≈ £25 inc VAT). A Lathams / supplier quote fixes this.
2. **Niche size.** Two 500 units need an opening of **≥ 1,006–1,010 mm** wide (fitting gaps), ≥ 506 high and ≥ 500
   deep — or fillers/scribes, which add time. Needs measuring on site.
3. **Hinge holes on the IDS door** — drilled by IDS (state 35 mm cup, 2 hinges) or by us (adds ~0.25 h).
4. **Who fits** — us (+ £90 + travel) or the client.
5. **Knobs** — model and who supplies.
6. **IDS lead time** (above).
