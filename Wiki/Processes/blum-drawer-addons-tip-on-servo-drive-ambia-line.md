# Blum drawer add-ons — TIP-ON, TIP-ON BLUMOTION, SERVO-DRIVE, AMBIA-LINE

**Owner, 2026-10-03: *"Let's do one more batch."*** These are the handle-less and convenience options for the drawer
systems already written up. Every figure is from the Blum 2022/2023 catalogue. *2022/2023 edition: check current data
before ordering.*

## 0. Which drawer system can have what

| System | TIP-ON (push to open) | TIP-ON BLUMOTION (push to open **and** soft close) | SERVO-DRIVE (electric) | AMBIA-LINE inserts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **METABOX** | **— none in the catalogue** | — | — | — |
| **LEGRABOX** | ✓ `750…T` profiles | ✓ standard `750…S` profiles + set | ✓ | ✓ |
| **MOVENTO** | ✓ `760H/766H…T` runners | ✓ standard `…S` runners + set | ✓ | — |
| **TANDEM** | *(not in R2)* | *(not in R2)* | ✓ | — |

**METABOX cannot be handle-less with Blum parts**: the METABOX chapter (B9) has no TIP-ON or SERVO-DRIVE at all. *Checked
by text search of the chapter, 2026-10-03.* For a handle-less drawer unit, use **LEGRABOX or MOVENTO**.

## 1. TIP-ON BLUMOTION — push to open, soft close

Gap **2.5 mm** (front gap template `65.5631`). **The cabinet profile or runner stays the standard soft-close one**
(`750.<NL>01S` for LEGRABOX, `760H<NL>0S` / `766H<NL>0S` for MOVENTO), with a **TIP-ON BLUMOTION set** added. The
set is chosen by NL and the **total weight of the pull-out**:

| NL | Unit | Profile/runner | Pull-out weight | Part no. |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 270–300 (MOVENTO 270–320) | S0 | 40 kg | ≤ 10 kg | `T60L7040` |
| 270–300 (MOVENTO 270–320) | S1 | 40 kg | > 10–20 kg | `T60L7140` |
| 350–600 | L1 | 40 kg only | ≤ 20 kg | `T60L7340` |
| 350–600 | L3 | 40 kg only | 15–40 kg | `T60L7540` |
| 450–650 (MOVENTO 450–750) | L5 | 70 kg (MOVENTO 60/70) | 35–70 kg | `T60L7570` |

LEGRABOX sink pull-outs have their own sets (`T60L9340`, `T60L9540`).

**Synchronisation** (Blum recommends it generally; it widens the trigger range). **Cut to the inside cabinet width LW**:

| | Narrow cabinets | Wider cabinets |
|---|---|---|
| **LEGRABOX** | LW 245–293: square sync `T60.300D`, cut **LW − 221** | LW ≥ 294: round linkage `T60.1125W` + 2 × adapter `T60.000D`, cut **LW − 247** (sink: LW − 267) |
| **MOVENTO** | LW 265–313: `T60.300D`, cut **LW − 241** | LW ≥ 314: `T60.1125W` + 2 × `T60.000D`, cut **LW − 267** |

The round linkage suits up to LW 1370. LEGRABOX note: the latch set `ZI7.0M07` cannot be combined with TIP-ON BLUMOTION.

## 2. TIP-ON — push to open, no soft close

Gap **3.5 mm** (LEGRABOX). **Different profile or runner part numbers, ending `T`**:

- **LEGRABOX:** `750.<NL>01T` (NL 270–600) / `753.<NL>01T` (450–650).
- **MOVENTO:** `760H<NL>0T` (NL 250–600) / `766H<NL>0T` (450–750).

Optional synchronisation: pinion set `T57.7400.01` + linkage `ZST.1160W`. Cut **LW − 229** (LEGRABOX), **LW − 249**
(MOVENTO); suits cabinet width up to 1400. **LEGRABOX:** recommended from KB 600 (possible from 400), and Blum also
recommends front stabilisation. MOVENTO side stabilisation `ZS7M400MU/600MU/750MU` is for wide drawers with short NL.

**Which to choose:** TIP-ON BLUMOTION is the better product (soft close as well) and keeps the standard profiles. Plain
TIP-ON is the cheaper option, but **the profiles themselves change**, so the order and the SmartCabinet rail row
change with them.

## 3. SERVO-DRIVE — electric opening

**Electric opening, soft close kept** (B8 for LEGRABOX / MERIVOBOX / TANDEMBOX; R4 for MOVENTO / TANDEM). The pull-outs
are unchanged. What it adds to the cabinet:

- A **vertical aluminium bracket profile** at the back, cut to **LH − 10** (inside cabinet height), e.g. `Z10T750AA`.
- **One drive unit per pull-out**, `Z10A3000.03`, clipped to the bracket profile.
- **Distance bumpers** on the fronts: Ø5 `993.0530` / Ø8 `993.0830.01`. 2 per front up to FH 300, 4 above (and 4 with
  70 kg LEGRABOX profiles).
- **Power:** 24 W power supply `Z10NE030x` (language pack) + mains flex (GB: `Z10M200B`) + distribution cable
  `Z10K800AE`. **A socket is needed in or behind the unit. That is an electrician's job, not the workshop's.**
- **LEGRABOX needs LW ≥ 267** (242–266 is not possible); the drive position differs by height (N: A 45 / E min. 61; M–F:
  A 45 / E min. 69).

**For SmartCabinet:** SERVO-DRIVE adds no machining shown on the pages read. **The bracket profile's depth must be
allowed for behind the drawers.** *Read the space-requirement drawing (cat. p. 362) for the actual job before
committing a cabinet depth.*

## 4. AMBIA-LINE — inner dividing for LEGRABOX

Frames, cutlery inserts and bottle holders that **sit inside LEGRABOX (and MERIVOBOX) drawers**: steel or wood design,
for drawers and high-fronted pull-outs (I1, cat. pp. 526–539). **No machining. Chosen per job by NL and drawer width**,
from the recommendation pages (cat. pp. 532, 537). **Not for METABOX or wooden TANDEM/MOVENTO drawers.** TANDEMBOX has its
own system, ORGA-LINE (I2).
