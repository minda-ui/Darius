# Blum TANDEM and MOVENTO runners — wooden drawers, drilling and SmartCabinet input

**Owner, 2026-10-02: *"Start with the runners."*** This covers the two Blum runners that go **under wooden
drawers**:

- **TANDEM 560H**: full extension with BLUMOTION, 30 kg, for **11–16 mm** drawer sides. The everyday choice.
- **MOVENTO 760H / 766H**: full extension with BLUMOTION S, **40 kg** (760H) or **60/70 kg** (766H), for drawer sides
  up to **16 mm**. The heavier, smoother runner.

**These fit SmartCabinet's *Guide Cassetto* table as it was designed**, which was built for runners under wooden
drawers. METABOX did not fit it the same way.

**Every figure is from the Blum 2022/2023 catalogue.** Values marked *(drawing)* were read off a rendered
drawing, and values marked *(derived)* were calculated from printed values. **Build one drawer and check it before
a batch.**

## 1. Part numbers

**TANDEM 560H, full extension, BLUMOTION, 30 kg.** Runners are ordered as a left/right pair. The part number is
`560H` + NL×10 + `B`:

| NL (mm) | 250 | 270 | 300 | 320 | 350 | 380 | 400 | 420 | 450 | 480 | 500 | 520 | 550 | 600 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Part no. | 560H2500B | 560H2700B | 560H3000B | 560H3200B | 560H3500B | 560H3800B | 560H4000B | 560H4200B | 560H4500B | 560H4800B | 560H5000B | 560H5200B | 560H5500B | 560H6000B |

**For 17–19 mm drawer sides use TANDEM 560F instead** (`560F2500B` … `560F6000B`, NL 250/270/300/350/400/450/500/550/600).
The catalogue marks it *on request*.

**MOVENTO 760H, 40 kg** (`760H` + NL×10 + `S`): NL **250, 270, 300, 320, 350, 380, 400, 420, 450, 480, 500, 520, 550,
600**, e.g. **`760H4500S`**. `760H2500S` is not compatible with TIP-ON BLUMOTION.
**MOVENTO 766H, 60/70 kg**: NL **450, 500, 520, 550, 580, 600, 650, 700, 750**, e.g. `766H5000S`.

**Ordered separately: you need these to fit the drawer.**

| Item | TANDEM 560H/560F | MOVENTO 760H/766H |
|---|---|---|
| **Locking devices**, left/right, one pair per drawer | **T51.1700.04** | **T51.7601** |
| Front adjuster (optional) | 295.1000 | — |
| Depth adjustment (optional; inset only) | POSISTOP 298.5500 is a depth stop | 298.7600 |
| Side stabilisation set, for wide, short drawers (up to 1400 mm cabinet width; cut to size) | ZST.410TV / ZST.600TV / ZST.750TV | ZS7M400MU / ZS7M600MU / ZS7M750MU |
| Drilling template for the drawer | **T65.1000.02** | **T65.1000.02** |

*No prices: the catalogue carries none.*

## 2. Drawer sizes

Both runners use the same rules:

| What | Rule | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Internal drawer width SKW** (between the drawer sides' inner faces) | **LW − 42** (+0 / −1.5) | printed; LW = internal cabinet width |
| Drawer side thickness | TANDEM H **11–16**; MOVENTO **max. 16** | printed |
| → **Outside width of the drawer box** | **LW − 42 + 2 × side thickness**; for 16 mm sides **LW − 10** | *(derived)* |
| **Drawer length SKL** | **NL − 10** | printed |
| Minimum internal cabinet depth | **NL + 3** | printed (+12 with side stabilisation) |
| **Base recess**: bottom edge of the drawer side to the underside of the base | **12–15 mm** | *(drawing)* |
| **Underside of the base** above the bottom of the runner | TANDEM **min. 27.5**; MOVENTO **min. 28.5** | *(drawing)* |
| Free space above the drawer side | **min. 7** | *(drawing)* |
| Inner drawer behind a front of thickness X | ISKL = SKL + X | printed |

**Choosing NL:** take the largest NL that is **no more than the internal cabinet depth − 3**. For example, a
560 mm-deep carcass with 18 mm sides and a 16 mm back gives a typical internal depth of ~544. NL ≤ 541, so
**NL 520** (both TANDEM and MOVENTO come in 520). *(derived — measure your carcass)*

## 3. Cabinet-side drilling — the runner

The runner's front fixing hole is on the **System 32 line, 37 mm from the cabinet front edge**, with a second hole
**32 mm** behind it, at **69**. A further hole sits 9 mm in front, at **28** *(drawing)*. The rear fixing depends on
NL. These are positions measured **from the cabinet front edge**, read off the hole-spacing drawings and converted
*(drawing)*:

| NL | TANDEM 560H holes (mm) | MOVENTO 760H holes (mm) |
|---|---|---|
| 250, 270 | 37, 69, 133, 197 | 37, 69, 197 |
| 300, 320, 350 | 37, 69, 165, 261 (380 too) | 37, 69, 261 |
| 380 | as 300–350 | 37, 69, 261, 293 |
| 400, 420 | 37, 69, 165, 229 | 37, 69, 261, 293 |
| 450, 480 | 37, 69, 165, 261 | 37, 69, 261, 293 |
| 500, 520 | 37, 69, 261, 275 | 37, 69, 261, 293 |
| 550 | 37, 69, 261, 275 | 37, 69, 261, 293 + one further back |
| 600 | 37, 69, 261, 275, 325 | as 550 |

**All of the TANDEM positions except 275 fall on the 32 mm grid from 37** (37 + 32·n), as do all the MOVENTO
positions. A Vitap System 32 line row can therefore pre-drill them. **Height of the hole line:** **37 mm** (TANDEM;
+3 with side stabilisation) or **min. 38 mm** (MOVENTO; +1 if the runners go on before the cabinet is assembled),
measured above the bottom of the runner *(drawing)*.

Screws: chipboard Ø3.5 (609.1500), Ø4, or system screws Ø6 × 14.5 (661.1450.HG) in Ø5 holes. The same fixings as
METABOX.

## 4. Drawer drilling

- **Drawer sides: no holes.** The locking devices clip on and are screwed **under the base at the front**. They sit
  **55 mm** (TANDEM) or **75 mm** (MOVENTO; 125 with TIP-ON BLUMOTION) back, positioned by template T65.1000.02
  *(drawing)*.
- **Rear: one Ø6 hole, 10 deep, each side**, for the runner's rear hook. It is drilled into the back of the drawer
  **7 mm** in from the side's outer face and **11 mm** up *(drawing — read from a perspective sketch; confirm with the
  template on the first drawer)*.

## 5. Into SmartCabinet — Tabelle CAM Accessori → Guide Cassetto

**Check first.** Open the drawer's **Ferramenta → Guide** list, filter by Blum, and see whether TANDEM or MOVENTO is
already there. Then try **Utilità → Import Accessori**. **If you have to enter them yourself, do it the way
LEGRABOX is entered: one accessory per NL under `25 GUIDE CASSETTO`**, coded by Blum part number (`560H4500B`,
`760H4500S` …). One row per NL is what lets SmartCabinet pick the runner automatically from the cabinet depth.

| Field | Meaning (SmartCabinet manual) | TANDEM 560H | MOVENTO 760H |
|---|---|---|---|
| `LX` | side clearance for the runner | **21 − side thickness** → **5** for 16 mm sides | **5** for 16 mm sides |
| `LY` | bottom of the drawer sides to the cabinet bottom | **27.5 − base recess** → **14.5** with a 13 mm recess | **28.5 − recess** → **15.5** |
| `CX` | notch width (some runners; 10 mm high) | **0** | **0** |
| `LBox` | drawer box depth, excluding the front | **NL − 10** | **NL − 10** |
| Side holes 1–6 (`X`, `Y`, `Ø`, `φ`) | holes in the drawer sides | **empty** | **empty** |
| Back holes (`X`, `Y`, `Ø`, `φ`) | holes in the drawer back, symmetric | **X 7, Y 11, Ø 6, φ 10** *(drawing)* | same |
| `DrawerGuideHoleBackOpposite` (CAM settings) | 0 = drill from inside, 1 = from outside | **1** (the hook enters from behind) — *check* | same |

*`LX` and `LY` are derived from Blum's printed rules (SKW = LW − 42; min. 27.5 / 28.5). **Whether SmartCabinet's
`LX` is per side or in total is not stated in the manual pages read.** Draw one drawer and check that the inside
width comes out at **LW − 42**: if it comes out at LW − 37, enter 10. The base recess is set in the drawer-box
configuration, not here.*

## 6. What is still open

- **Whether TANDEM or MOVENTO is already in SmartCabinet**, or in Import Accessori. **Check first** (§5).
- **Which runner the shop actually uses**, and its drawer side thickness (16 or 18). That decides 560H or 560F, and
  `LX`.
- `LX` per side or total: check with one drawer (§5).
- The rear Ø6 hole position: confirm with template T65.1000.02 on the first drawer.
- **Whether SmartCabinet drills the cabinet-side runner holes at all.** The *Guide Cassetto* table only describes
  drawer holes, so check one cabinet's CN output. This is the same open question as for METABOX.
