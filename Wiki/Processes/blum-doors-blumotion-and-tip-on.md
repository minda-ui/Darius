# Blum doors — soft close (BLUMOTION) and push-to-open (TIP-ON)

**Owner, 2026-10-03: *"Move to next."*** After AVENTOS, the next Blum topic is door motion. Every figure is from the
Blum 2022/2023 catalogue; **values marked *(drawing)* were read off a rendered drawing.** *2022/2023 edition: check
current data before ordering.*

## 1. Soft close — the shop already has it

The hinges in the shop's SmartCabinet (`71B3550` overlay, `71B3650` half overlay, `71B3750` inset; see
`blum-clip-top-hinges-smartcabinet-input.md`) are **CLIP top BLUMOTION**, with BLUMOTION built into the hinge (and
the option to switch it off). **Nothing extra is needed for soft close on doors.**

The separate BLUMOTION units in chapter H3 (`973A` clip-on, `971A` in an adapter plate, `970A`/`970.` drilled) are only
for hinges **without** built-in BLUMOTION: glass doors, mini-hinges, aluminium frames and the corner bi-fold hinge
(`970.1002` with `79T8500`). Overview: cat. p. 158.

*Blum's note (cat. p. 74): mixing CLIP top BLUMOTION hinges with sprung CLIP top hinges on one door is only for small,
light fronts up to 300 mm wide, after a trial. Not recommended on wider fronts.*

## 2. The three versions of each hinge — why it matters for TIP-ON

Each CLIP top 110° hinge comes in three versions. Screw-on boss, from cat. p. 74:

| Application | **BLUMOTION** (soft close) | **Sprung** (self-closing) | **Unsprung** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overlay | `71B3550` | `71T3550` | `70T3550.TL` |
| Half overlay | `71B3650` | `71T3650` | `70T3650.TL` |
| Inset | `71B3750` | `71T3750` | `70T3750.TL` |

INSERTA versions: `71B3590` / `71T3590` / `70T3590.TL` (and `…3690`, `…3790`). **All three versions drill the same**:
Ø35 cup, same plate. **Only the hinge part number changes.**

**TIP-ON needs a different hinge from the shop's usual one.** Blum's TIP-ON table on the hinge page pairs TIP-ON only with
CLIP top hinges **with or without spring**, not with CLIP top BLUMOTION. *A soft-close hinge pulls the door shut; TIP-ON
pushes it open.* So a handle-less job means **ordering the sprung or unsprung hinge** instead of the `71B…`.

## 3. TIP-ON for doors — which unit

| Unit | Use with | Fronts | Stroke | Hole | Part no. |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TIP-ON 956 short, magnet** | **unsprung** hinges `70T…` | overlay, up to ~1300 high | ~17 mm | **Ø10, min. 50 deep** | `956.1004` |
| **TIP-ON 956A long, magnet** | **unsprung** hinges | overlay from ~1300 high; **all inset doors** | ~38 mm | **Ø10, min. 76 deep** | `956A1004` |
| **TIP-ON 956A long, bumper** | **sprung** hinges `71T…` | any | ~38 mm | Ø10, min. 76 deep | `956A1006` (strong spring `956A1006F`) |

- Colours: SW silk white · CS carbon black · R7036 platinum grey. The colour is chosen when ordering (cat. p. 172).
- **Magnet units come with two catch plates for the door**: glue-on (not for powder-coated, waxed or oiled surfaces) or
  screw-on (Ø15, set **3 mm off-centre**, a chipboard screw Ø3.5 × 15). The bumper version needs none (Blum suggests
  one on very soft surfaces).
- **Strong spring** (`956A1006F`): for narrow fronts with many hinges.
- **Front gap** *(drawing)*: magnet versions min. **1.5** (2.6 with the glue-on plate, 3.1 with the screw-on one).
  Bumper version min. **3.2**. Every unit adjusts **+4 / −1 mm**.

## 4. Two ways to fit it

**a) Drilled in.** A **Ø10 (+0.1) blind hole, 50 deep (short) or 76 deep (long), into the front edge of the cabinet**
(side, top or bottom panel), and the unit pressed in. *(drawing)* **On the shop's Vitap K2 as read on 2026-09-23 this is
probably not possible.** The head's Ø10 is a **vertical** blind drill (bush 4), and no Ø10 is listed among the horizontal
positions. A 76 mm horizontal depth is also more than any edge bore in the jobs reviewed so far (Ø8 × 30). *That the
41–54 bushes are the horizontal ones is my inference from the layout.* **Drill by hand with Blum's template (cat.
p. 666), or use (b).**

**b) In an adapter plate**, screwed to the inside of the cabinet with chipboard screws Ø3.5 or Ø4 (system screws as
an alternative). **No edge boring.** *(drawing)*

| Adapter plate | For | Screw holes from the cabinet front edge | Part no. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Straight, short, **20/17** | `956.1004` | 20 and 37 | `956.1201` |
| Straight, extended, **20/32** | `956A…` | 20 and 52 | `956A1201` |
| Cruciform, extended, **37/32** | `956A…` | on the **37** line, two holes **32** apart | `956A1501` |

The **37/32 cruciform plate uses the same hole line as the hinge mounting plates**. It is the easiest to put into the
drilling program (two Ø5 holes on the System 32 line, or just screws). Positioning template for the catch plate: cat. p. 687.

## 5. SmartCabinet

**The SmartCabinet manual pages read so far (*Ante → Ferramenta*) list handles, hinges, AVENTOS, sliding kits and
reinforcing bars. TIP-ON is not named.** Until the shop's SmartCabinet shows otherwise:

- Design the door **without a handle**, with the **sprung or unsprung hinge** entered in the hinge table. Duplicate
  the existing `71B…` *Cerniere* row under the new code: the drilling is identical.
- Fit TIP-ON by **adapter plate** (§4b). With the cruciform plate, the two holes sit on the 37/32 line. Add them in
  TpaCAD if the shop wants them pre-drilled.
- **Check Import Accessori** for Blum TIP-ON, as for METABOX and AVENTOS, before entering anything by hand.
