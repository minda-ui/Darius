# Blum AVENTOS lift systems — choosing, drilling and SmartCabinet input

**Owner, 2026-10-03: *"Do AVENTOS next."*** AVENTOS is the set of **lift mechanisms for wall-cabinet flaps that open
upwards**. Every figure is from the Blum 2022/2023 catalogue. **Values marked *(drawing)* were read off a rendered
drawing.** Drill and hang one sample before a batch. *2022/2023 edition: check current data before ordering.*

## 1. Which AVENTOS — by how the front opens

| System | Front | Cabinet height KH | Width KB | Chosen by | Hinges? | Cat. page |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HK top** | one front, **tilts up** (stay lift) | **205–600** | ≤ 1800 | power factor LF | **none** | 44 |
| **HK-S** | small stay lift | ≤ 600 | — | LF | none | 54 |
| **HK-XS** | small stay lift, **shallow cabinets** (LT ≥ 125, or 100) | 240–600 | — | LF | **yes, CLIP top** | 60 |
| **HL** | one front, **lifts straight up** parallel to the cabinet | **300–580** | ≤ 1800 | KH + front weight table | none | 38 |
| **HS** | one front, **up and over** the cabinet top | **350–800** | ≤ 1800 | KH + front weight table | none | 32 |
| **HF** | **two fronts folding up** (bi-fold) | **480–1040** | ≤ 1800 | LF of both fronts | **yes: 2 top + 2 centre** | 20 |

**Power factor LF = cabinet height KH (mm) × front weight FG (kg)**, the front weight **including handles** (for HK:
*"incl. double handle weight"*). For HF, FG is the **two fronts together**. Front weights per m²: catalogue p. 704.
**A third mechanism raises LF by 50 %** (HK top, HK-S). Blum recommends one on wide cabinets so the middle of the
front does not sag. **HK top: max. FG 18 kg on two mechanisms.** HK-XS: LF doubles when used on both sides.

INSERTA set: `78Z5530T11`. **3 hinges from KB 1200 or FG 12 kg; 4 from KB 1800 or FG 20 kg.**

> **Correction to an earlier reply (2026-10-03):** I said the shop's SmartCabinet hinge **`79T8500`** goes with AVENTOS HF.
> **It does not.** Blum (catalogue p. 114) lists `79T8500` as the **corner-cabinet bi-fold hinge**, used with a

## 3. Drilling — cabinet sides

All from the cabinet's **front edge**. Hole heights are **from the top edge of the side** unless stated otherwise.
*(drawing)*

| System | Holes | Height | Hole | Fixing |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HK top** (system screws) | **37 · 69 · 133 · 165** (37, +32, +64, +32) | **36** below the top | **Ø5 × 11.5** | pre-mounted system screws |
| HK top (chipboard screws) | positioning system, no pre-drilling shown | — | — | 4 × Ø4 × 35 |
| **HL** | **37 · 229** (37 + 192) | **88 + SOB** below the top | Ø5 × 5 | 5 × Ø4 × 35 |
| **HS** | **37 · 229** | **80 + SOB** below the top | Ø5 × 5 | 5 × Ø4 × 35 |
| **HF** | **37 · 229** | **H** below the top: KH 480–549 → **KH × 0.3 − 28**; KH 550–1040 → **KH × 0.3 − 57** | Ø5 × 5 | 4 × Ø4 × 35 |
| **HK-S** | peg holes **37 · 101** (37 + 64) | **74** below the top (top panel ≤ 22) | Ø5, min. 5 deep | + 3 × Ø4 × 35 (p. 55) |
| **HK-XS** | two holes **32 apart**, **37** from the front (45 for a 100-deep cabinet) | **H = 137 + MD + K + SOB** (MD = mounting plate spacing; K = hinge arm crank: 0 / 9.5 / 18) | — | p. 62 |

SOB = top panel thickness. The SERVO-DRIVE cable holes (Ø10) are only needed with SERVO-DRIVE.

**Space:** HK top needs **inside height ≥ 173** and depth ≥ 187 (261 with a visible wall-hanging bracket). HL needs
inside height LH ≥ 262 / 312 / 362 / 412 (by lever arm) and depth ≥ 278. HS needs depth ≥ 276. HF needs depth ≥ 278. HK-XS needs inside height ≥ 200 and
depth ≥ 125. *(drawing)*

## 4. Drilling — the front

- **HK top**, wooden fronts: front fixing bracket on each side, **4 chipboard screws** at **62 + FAo, then +32, +32,
  +32** down from the front's top edge, **SFA + 12.5** in from the side edge. FAo = upper front overlay (max. 25.4);
  SFA = side overlay. *(drawing)*
- **HF**: the arm's front bracket sits on the **lower front**, **X below its mid-height**, **SFA + 12.5** in from the side.
  X depends on KH and the mounting plate: KH 480–549 → **70** (68 with `175H5400`); KH 550–1040 → **47** (45). The top
  front hangs on CLIP top 120° hinges: drilling distance **TB 3–6**, from the TB table on p. 22. The centre hinges join
  the two fronts. *(drawing)*
- **HL, HS, HK-S, HK-XS**: front brackets on pp. 40, 34, 55, 62. **Not tabulated here yet.**

## 5. SmartCabinet — *Tabelle CAM Accessori → Supporto Anta (Aventos)*

From the manual. The AVENTOS item must exist first in **Anagrafica Accessori**. **Try Import Accessori first**, as with
METABOX: Kosmosoft may already ship Blum AVENTOS rows. Each row in the table is **one hole**, drilled **on both sides**
(symmetrically):

| Column | For the cabinet side (**S**) | For the front (**D**) |
|---|---|---|
| `S/D` | S | D |
| `X` | horizontal distance **from the front edge** | vertical distance **from the top edge** (positive) or **from the bottom edge** (negative) |
| `Y` | vertical distance **from the top edge** | horizontal distance **from the side edges** |
| `Ø` / `φ` | diameter / depth | diameter / depth |
| `Min H Anta` / `Max H Anta` | the front-height range the row applies to. **Outside it, the system is not applied** | same |

**For a double front (HF), the D holes are drilled on the lower front.** In the door's *Ferramenta* tab the AVENTOS
system becomes available once the door opens **up** (or down). Traditional hinges can be added as well. **For a double
front the hinges joining the two fronts are compulsory.**

**HK top, system-screw version: worked example of the S rows** *(from §3; for a sample cabinet before production)*:

| S/D | X | Y | Ø | φ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S | 37 | 36 | 5 | 11.5 |
| S | 69 | 36 | 5 | 11.5 |
| S | 133 | 36 | 5 | 11.5 |
| S | 165 | 36 | 5 | 11.5 |

**`Y` = 36 assumes Blum's "top edge" is the top of the side panel.** Check it on the first sample. The front rows (D)
depend on FAo, which changes with the top-panel construction. The Ø of the chipboard-screw pilot holes is the shop's
choice: Blum gives positions, not pilot sizes.

**Where formulas are involved, the table cannot follow them.** It has fixed X/Y per row, and only height limits.

- HF's **H = KH × 0.3 − 28/57** needs one row set per cabinet height band.
- HK-XS's **137 + MD + K + SOB** likewise needs a row set for each combination.
- Use **`Min H Anta` / `Max H Anta`** to split the bands where the front height tracks KH.
