# EGGER compact laminates

**Owner, 2026-10-04: *"yes, go through the Egger compact laminates"*.** This article covers what the brochure says
and what it means for this shop. **It is a manufacturer's brochure, not a processing manual.** EGGER puts the
binding rules in separate *processing instructions* (`to.egger.link/processing-instructions-compact-laminates`),
**not read yet**. The brochure itself says its technical chapter "cannot serve as instructions for use".

## 1. What it is

**Solid HPL** (high-pressure laminate), **2 mm and thicker**: resin-impregnated kraft papers pressed with a decor
paper on **both faces**. It is homogeneous right through, so **it needs no edging**. Three kinds:

| Kind | Standard | Core | Thicknesses (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Black core** | EN 438-4, CGS (ACS pearlescent) | black | 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 13 |
| **Coloured core** | EN 438-9, BCS | white, light grey, dark grey (matched to decor) | 6 / 8 / 10 / 13 |
| **Flammex** (fire-retardant black core) | CGF / ACF | black | 6 / 8 / 10 |

**Boards: 5,600 or 2,790 × 2,060.** Ours run 2,800 × 2,070, so the 2,790 board is **10 mm shorter and narrower**:
**re-check nesting**. **Worktops: 12 mm, 4,100 × 650 / 920 / 1,300**, pre-bevelled top and bottom on the long edges.

Properties claimed: moisture-resistant, impact- and scratch-resistant, food-safe, antibacterial (ISO 22196),
stain- and chemical-resistant, low-emission.

## 3. Worktops (12 mm) — the rules that affect our base units

- **It moves.** Allow **2 mm per metre** dilation. Movement across the board is about **twice** that along it.
- **Carcass tops must be open (rails), not closed.** *"Closed body tops are not permitted and can cause the
  boards to warp."* Our base units already use **top rails** (BU60: 2 × 150 rails) ✓.
- **Sink and hob units: fit metal crossbars** to stiffen the top where cut-outs weaken it.
- **Fixing:** flat-head screws **up through the crossbar**, the hole in the crossbar **larger than the screw**.
  Or glue with a stress-equalising adhesive (Innotec Adheseal / Powerbond XS 330, Jowat 690.00, SikaTack
  Panel, Ottocoll M500 / M560). Gluing leaves a **~3 mm shadow gap**.
- **Cut-outs:**
  - corner radius **≥ 5 mm** (undermount sinks);
  - flush-mounted sink or hob: leave **≥ 6 mm** of thickness;
  - remaining strip **≥ 50 mm** anywhere;
  - **≥ 300 mm** from a corner joint;
  - hob to tall unit **≥ 300 mm**;
  - the hob must not rest on the cut edge (up to 150 °C).
- **Joints:**
  - special **12 mm worktop connectors** (EGGER set), pocket **8 mm deep**;
  - **2 connectors up to 799 wide, 3 from 800**;
  - spring guides or lamellos centred in the thickness;
  - EGGER sealant in the joint.
- **Carry cut worktops upright.** Once cut, they snap easily.

## 4. Table tops and shelves — max overhang and support spacing (p. 37)

| Thickness | Max projection | Max mounting distance |
|---|---|---|
| 10 mm (minimum for tops) | 100 | 310 |
| 12 mm | 150 | 390 |
| 13 mm | 200 | 440 |

Fixing holes in the frame **2–3 mm bigger** than the screw. Assemble **stress-free**.

## 5. Doors

- **No wider than high**, and **cut lengthways along the board**, because the board moves less in that direction.
- Top and bottom hinges **within 100 mm** of the edge. For heavy use add a hinge **≤ 100 mm below the top one**.
  **Trial-fit first.**
- **Thin-door hinges:** Blum **EXPANDO T**, Hettich Sensys thin-door, Häfele HPL hinge, Prämeta 3000.
  ***Our usual CLIP top 71B3550 drills a Ø35 × 13 cup, which is too deep for a thin compact door.*** *That is
  my reading; the brochure doesn't list cup depths.*

## 6. Machining and health

- **"Can be milled, grooved and drilled … on the surface or in the core"**; tapped holes are possible. The
  brochure gives **no cutting data**, which is in the processing instructions (not read). *Not yet known for our
  machines:* the right saw blade for the **F45** and **Vitap**, and feeds and speeds.
- **Dust:** a sensitiser, and a fire and explosion risk. **Use effective extraction**, with breathing protection if
  extraction isn't adequate. *Relevant to `FA2402` and its open fault history* (`troubleshooting-aes-saf-10000.md`).

## 7. Wall cladding and wet areas (for reference)

Minimum **8 mm**. **Ventilated** substructure. **≥ 50 mm** off the floor (120 mm in commercial sanitary use).
Gap at the ceiling. Corrosion-free fixings. Direct-to-wall fixing only up to **300 mm** high, with damp proofing.

## 8. Not in the brochure

- **Prices** (none in the Panel Price Library either; Lathams have never quoted compact laminate).
- **Cutting and tooling data.** Fetch the processing instructions before the first job.
