# EGGER compact laminates

**Owner, 2026-10-04: *"yes, go through the Egger compact laminates"*.** This article covers what the brochure says
and what it means for this shop. **The brochure is backed by EGGER's processing instructions and the Leitz and Leuco tool guides, read on
2026-10-04** (§6). The brochure itself says its technical chapter "cannot serve as instructions for use".

## 1. What it is

**Solid HPL** (high-pressure laminate), **2 mm and thicker**: resin-impregnated kraft papers pressed with a decor
paper on **both faces**. It is homogeneous right through, so **it needs no edging**. Three kinds:

| Kind | Standard | Core | Thicknesses (mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Black core** | EN 438-4, CGS (ACS pearlescent) | black | 3 / 4 / 5 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 13 |
| **Coloured core** | EN 438-9, BCS | white, light grey, dark grey (matched to decor) | 6 / 8 / 10 / 13 |
| **Flammex** (fire-retardant black core) | CGF / ACF | black | 6 / 8 / 10 |

**Boards: 5,600 or 2,790 × 2,060.** Ours run 2,800 × 2,070, so the 2,790 board is **10 mm shorter and narrower**:
**re-check nesting**. **Worktops: 12 mm, 4,100 × 650 / 920 / 1,300**, pre-bevelled top and bottom on the long edges.

Properties claimed: moisture-resistant, impact- and scratch-resistant, food-safe, antibacterial (ISO 22196),
stain- and chemical-resistant, low-emission.

## 3. Worktops (12 mm) — the rules that affect our base units

- **It moves.** Allow **2 mm per metre** dilation. Movement across the board is about **twice** that along it.
- **Carcass tops must be open (rails), not closed.** *"Closed body tops are not permitted and can cause the
  boards to warp."* Our base units already use **top rails** (BU60: 2 × 150 rails) ✓.
- **Sink and hob units: fit metal crossbars** to stiffen the top where cut-outs weaken it.
- **Fixing:** flat-head screws **up through the crossbar**, the hole in the crossbar **larger than the screw**.
  Or glue with a stress-equalising adhesive (Innotec Adheseal / Powerbond XS 330, Jowat 690.00, SikaTack
  Panel, Ottocoll M500 / M560). Gluing leaves a **~3 mm shadow gap**.
- **Cut-outs:**
  - corner radius **≥ 5 mm** (undermount sinks);
  - flush-mounted sink or hob: leave **≥ 6 mm** of thickness;
  - remaining strip **≥ 50 mm** anywhere;
  - **≥ 300 mm** from a corner joint;
  - hob to tall unit **≥ 300 mm**;
  - the hob must not rest on the cut edge (up to 150 °C).
- **Joints:**
  - special **12 mm worktop connectors** (EGGER set), pocket **8 mm deep**;
  - **2 connectors up to 799 wide, 3 from 800**;
  - spring guides or lamellos centred in the thickness;
  - EGGER sealant in the joint.
- **Carry cut worktops upright.** Once cut, they snap easily.

## 4. Table tops and shelves — max overhang and support spacing (p. 37)

| Thickness | Max projection | Max mounting distance |
|---|---|---|
| 10 mm (minimum for tops) | 100 | 310 |
| 12 mm | 150 | 390 |
| 13 mm | 200 | 440 |

Fixing holes in the frame **2–3 mm bigger** than the screw. Assemble **stress-free**.

## 5. Doors

- **No wider than high**, and **cut lengthways along the board**, because the board moves less in that direction.
- Top and bottom hinges **within 100 mm** of the edge. For heavy use add a hinge **≤ 100 mm below the top one**.
  **Trial-fit first.**
- **Thin-door hinges:** Blum **EXPANDO T**, Hettich Sensys thin-door, Häfele HPL hinge, Prämeta 3000.
  ***Our usual CLIP top 71B3550 drills a Ø35 × 13 cup, which is too deep for a thin compact door.*** *That is
  my reading; the brochure doesn't list cup depths.*

## 6. Machining — from EGGER's processing instructions and the Leitz / Leuco tool guides

**Sources, filed 2026-10-04 in `Raw/EGGER/`** (Drive folder `167yUHu3BCw1p_FxnfTVtZUv7gWWZheSn`; each upload's md5
matched the downloaded file). Download chain: `www.egger.com` → `binary.egger.link` → `downloads.egger-cdn.com`.
- **Processing instructions, Compact Laminates**, rev. 04, 24 June 2026, 24 pp. (`1viLKuzTpheuWmzrK71IKhfH8dlLCNAVr`);
- **Machining recommendation, Leitz**, version 01/2026, 21 pp. (`1qnex6Atz-se2i_jIPUwWHOeY8bICGaaQ`). Its tables are
  images, read by OCR, so **check any figure against the PDF before ordering**;
- **Tool recommendation, Leuco**, 9 pp. (`1A7O24ksQHTclCfBJyYWbB4dExDLP3-Ly`);
- **Processing instructions, EGGER Worktops**, 27 pp. (`1VWdWdWfp4aM4R1k8jd8Mo4Xcxa3i_0e0`).

### Sawing — the F45

- **Decor side up.** Use the **scorer** for a clean underside.
- **Blade height decides the edge.** Rough **top** edge: raise the blade. Rough **underside**: lower it. Find the best
  setting by test.
- **Diamond (DP) blades are recommended; carbide (HW) is "suitable to a limited extent".** Avoid segmented blades.
  Tooth shapes: **TR/TR** (trapezoid / flat-trapezoid) or **FZ/TR**; Leuco: HR / HR-TR / TR-F-FA / G6.
- **Cutting speed 60–90 m/s.** Leitz table 60–75, Leuco 55–80; take the **upper value with DP**. On the F45:
  - **Ø350 at 4,000 rpm = 73 m/s ✓**; at **5,000 rpm = 92 m/s**, just over;
  - Leuco's sizing-saw setting: **5,000–6,000 rpm, manual feed 4–6 m/min**.
  - *The fitted blade's diameter isn't recorded in the KB; its kerf measured 3.2 on 2026-10-04.*
- **Blade projection: the two guides disagree.**
  - Leitz, panel sizing saws: **5–10 mm**.
  - Leuco, sizing (sliding-table) saws: **15–20 mm**; panel saws by diameter: Ø300 15–25, **Ø350 18–28**, Ø400 25–30,
    Ø450 25–33.
  - **Find it by test on an offcut**, using the edge rule above.
- **Leitz blades that suit a table saw** (Diamaster PRO DZ/TR, DP, bore 30): **Ø303 × 3.2, Z60 or Z96**; **Ø350 × 3.5,
  Z72**. **DP scorer** (KON/FZ): match its width to the main blade.
  - Altendorf isn't in Leitz's machine list, so **check bore and pin holes with the F45 spare-parts manual**
    (scorer RAPIDO Ø180 in our KB).
- **Feed per tooth** 0.02–0.1 mm (Leitz) / aim 0.07–0.08 (Leuco, panel saws). A slow hand feed with a many-tooth
  blade rubs rather than cuts, which heats the edge and smells.

### Drilling — the Vitap K2 and hand drills

- **Bits for plastics** are best. **Leitz HW-solid Z2** (Ø3–10, break-out-free) or Leuco HL / Mosquito for through
  holes. Wood or metal twist bits work at **lower speed and feed**.
- **Leuco settings:**
  - blind holes: **4,500 rpm, 1.5–2 m/min**;
  - through holes: **4,000 rpm, 1.5 m/min**, slowing before breakthrough, **with peck/retract above 12 mm**;
  - **hinge cups: Leuco "Light" cylinder boring bits, 4,500 rpm, 1.5 m/min.**
- **Through holes:** back the board on something you can drill into.
- **Residual material: blind holes leave ≥ 1.5 mm**; holes parallel to the face leave **≥ 3 mm** top and bottom.

### Screws and fixings

- **Always pre-drill.** Threads can be tapped. Fine-pitch self-tapping screws hold best.
- Heavy pull-out loads: **RAMPA screw-in sleeves** (type ES; type E from 6 mm).
- Into the edge: **≥ 25 mm screw-in depth**, with the hole sized so it doesn't split.
- **Through fixings:**
  - hole **2–3 mm bigger** than the screw;
  - **flat-head, not countersunk** (a countersink locks the panel);
  - **one fixed point** (hole = screw), the rest **sliding points**, and **+2 mm per metre** from the fixed point.
- Max fixing spacing (indoor cladding), thickness → a × b: **8 → 790 × 500, 10 → 920 × 670, 12 → 960 × 900,
  13 → 970 × 920.**
- *Not addressed by EGGER:* **Cabineo X**, our carcass connector, which is made for chipboard. **Don't assume it
  works in compact laminate.**
  **Confirmat doesn't suit it either**: the shop's **5 × 50** screws leave 3.5 mm per side in a 12 mm edge, which
  passes EGGER's 3 mm, but the **coarse chipboard thread** is the wrong type for HPL
  (`carcase-fixings-cabineo-x-vs-confirmat.md`, 2026-10-04; corrected the same day from a 7 mm assumption).

### Cut-outs, joints, gluing

- **Inside corners R ≥ 5 mm**, and grooves and rebates rounded too, or the board cracks. Rout them, or drill the
  corners and saw between.
- Join parts **with the same production direction**. **Mark the running direction on offcuts.**
- Corners: grooved, tongue and groove, angle spring, metal profile, glue plus fixings.
- Adhesives (stress-equalising): Ottocoll M500 / M560, Adheseal Project, Powerbond XS 330, Jowat 690.00,
  SikaTack Panel. **Mitre / upstand:** Ottocoll P85 / P86, Würth PUR Rapid.
- **Full-face bonding to laminate: not recommended.**
- **Edges:** sand out tool marks, and **silicone-free oil** (Innoplast Protector, linseed) evens the colour.
  **Round sharp corners.**

### Storage and handling

- **Flat on a full-surface board, with a decor board top and bottom**, or **at about 80°** against a full support.
  Never on the floor or in sun.
- **Acclimatise at the place of use.** **Lift, don't slide**, faces against each other.
- **Tools wear faster** than in chipboard. For volume or CNC use, **diamond**.

### Dust

A **skin and respiratory sensitiser**, and a **fire and explosion risk**. **Effective extraction is required**; wear
breathing protection if extraction isn't adequate. *Relevant to `FA2402` and its fault history*
(`../Troubleshooting/troubleshooting-aes-saf-10000.md`).

### Doors

EGGER's guide values are **for a 13 mm door up to 600 mm wide** (Fig. 39 in the instructions). Extra hinge ≤ 100 below
the top one. Thin-door hinges: Prämeta 3000, **Blum EXPANDO T**, Hettich Sensys, Häfele.

### Worktop instructions — what they add

- **Drill holes ≥ 0.5 mm bigger** than the screw (e.g. 3.5 screw → ≥ 4 hole), deburred.
- Pipes through the top need **2–3 mm** clearance.
- 12 mm compact worktop: **overhang ≤ 150, support spacing ≤ 390** (same as §4).
- **Open end shelves in base units must be supported at the sides.**

## 7. Wall cladding and wet areas (for reference)

Minimum **8 mm**. **Ventilated** substructure. **≥ 50 mm** off the floor (120 mm in commercial sanitary use).
Gap at the ceiling. Corrosion-free fixings. Direct-to-wall fixing only up to **300 mm** high, with damp proofing.
