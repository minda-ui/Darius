# Häfele GOLA profiles

**Owner, 2026-10-04:** *"For gola I would prefer Hafele."* First use: the LEGO drawer unit (change log 2026-10-05).
*Häfele's own catalogue says "Dimensional data not binding".* The UK site is Cloudflare-blocked to this
environment, so **this catalogue extract is the source**.

**The connector that fixes a profile to the cabinet side is the red plastic B/C connector, `712.63.511` (screw) or
`712.63.521` (dowel)** — the same part for Original and Plus (p. 4.243 shows it under C Plus).

## What the carcase needs (p. 4.241 drawings, *the arithmetic and reading are mine*)

- **A notch in the front edge of every side and centre panel** where a profile runs: **C: 75 high × 26 deep, R5 inside
  corners** (Original; C Plus is 73 × 26, so assume **73–75 × 26**). **B (top): 57.5 × 26** (B Plus 56.5 × 27).
- **Connector holes** on the inside face, drawn at **32 mm** vertical spacing, about **24** from the notch; *Ø5 × 10*
  for the dowel version. **Read off small drawings — check against a connector in hand before programming.**
- **Machining:** all notches and holes on the Vitap (shop rule, 2026-10-04). A 26-deep notch is a routing job — the
  Vitap's Ø10 cutter at 101 — **not yet programmed or tested**.

## What the catalogue does NOT give

- **How far the fronts overlap the profile**, i.e. the **finger opening** at each channel. The section drawings show the
  fronts covering part of the profile's lips, but **no figure**. *This decides the front heights.*
  **Measure it** with an offcut of profile and a front, or ask Häfele technical (0800 171 2908).
- **Cutting:** aluminium, chop saw with an aluminium blade (p. 4.246 note).
