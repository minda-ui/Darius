- [SmartCabinet's online manual — map, and what it settles about T016](Software/smartcabinet-online-manual.md) - the vendor's online manual at `smartcabinet.eu/manuale/` (Italian, **no English edition**), mapped 2026-09-27 from ten pages fetched and hashed. **The design side had no manual at all until now.** Settles that T016's "systemic fix" rests on a false premise: SmartCabinet's tool record has **no ID field**, and for drilling it names no tool in the normal case — *"le punte a forare vengono normalmente selezionate automaticamente"* — so `#1001=0` on every hole in the master is the expected output, not a defect. **But the tool table's own first line says drill bits must be named *"per alcune macchine"***, so whether ours is one of those machines is the open question, not a settled no. Still open and labelled: whether the Vitap's postprocessor exposes the drilling-tool table, whose `tipo di foro` field might let blind/through be declared at the design side. Also: where CN files are written (`\Kosmosoft\SmartCabinet\CNC\`, subfolders named after the postprocessor), the `AutoCopyCNFolder` setting that can write them straight to the machine PC, and that **only `Gen 1` in the postprocessor list means the CN module is not licensed**. Text of the four CN/tool-chain pages captured in `../Outputs/2026-09-27-smartcabinet-manual-capture-cn-chain.md`
- [Can a project be rebuilt in SmartCabinet from its `.TCN` files?](Software/tcn-to-smartcabinet-reversibility.md) - **no as a project, yes as a set of parts**, answered 2026-09-29 by decoding the master's own two `.TCN` files. A CN file carries the blank (`DL`/`DH`/`DS`), the machined face and every operation exactly — and **discards everything about the cabinet**. The finding is that the format *has* the slots and the postprocessor writes them empty: `VAR`, `SPEC`, `OPTI`, **`LINK`** and `PREV` are all present and blank in both files, so an empty `LINK{}` is a deliberate silence rather than a format limitation. `$=SmartCabinet` is the whole provenance record — it names the program, not the project, unit or job — and the part names are a settable `CN Names` convention, not data. **The files are not even self-contained**: four workings call `..\custom\mcr\fittingx.tmcr`, a macro on the machine's PC. No SmartCabinet import is documented (inbound pages are only `nuovo`/`apri`; exports are CSV, DXF views, OBJ) — *but the manual's hub is not a complete index, so that is undocumented rather than proven absent*. What the files **are** good for: re-cutting any panel with no CAD involved, recovering every dimension, inferring the carcase from a full set, and reverse-engineering detail.
