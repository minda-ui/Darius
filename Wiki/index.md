- [Closing T016: the Blind-bore tool-ID fix, and a correction to what T016 was said to be](Processes/tpacad-blind-bore-tool-id-fix.md) - **the fix carried in the task note and `CLAUDE.md` §7 pointed at the wrong computer.** The Vitap is not missing a Ø5 tool — it has **five**, Blind Ø5 on bushes 6-10, all at **ID 0**, so diameter+type resolution has five candidates and no tie-break. The fix is two steps at the Vitap (give one bush a real ID; set the failing operation's Tool field to it), not a SmartCabinet catalog row — the 2026-09-15 at-the-machine record says the two are **unrelated**. Includes the verification test, why the Through-bore workaround must not be used to close it, and what the CN Tools row *is* for (the systemic fix: getting the post-processor to emit a Tool ID)
- [Making holes with a cutter instead of a drill (TpaCAD interpolation)](Processes/tpacad-interpolated-holes.md) - shop working note: toolpath circle = hole Ø − cutter Ø, so a 10 mm cutter makes Ø12 on a 2 mm orbit and Ø10 on a straight plunge; the unresolved `Diameter`-field convention means a wrong entry gives 22 mm instead of 12, which makes this the cheapest case in which to settle it for good; also corrects T027 — the shop **does** have a 35 mm drilling head
- [The panel production route (design → cut → edge → drill → assemble)](Processes/panel-production-route.md) - the first record in this KB of the order parts move through the shop: SmartCABINET optimisation → F45 cut → **F4 edging** → Vitap drill → assembly desk (owner, 2026-09-19); closes T028, and opens the question that edging *before* drilling forces — whether the cutting list's dimensions are cut sizes or finished sizes, which is systematic across every edged part; also makes the serial-line/single-point-of-failure position concrete and gives the costing model its skeleton
- [Reference: Altendorf F45 electrical schematics (FA2303)](Processes/f45-electrical-schematics-reference.md) - what Altendorf's own circuit diagrams (drawing `B1434.0220`, 17.10.2022) say about `FA2303` and what they do not; **the extraction-interlock terminals exist** (T018 becomes a site check), safety-chain device tags for three E-stops, the motor over-temperature terminals — but **no serial number** (T009 still open) and **no resistance value** (T011); poor scan, no wire traceable end to end
- [Process: SmartCabinet Wall Support Cam Table — column reference and worked example](Processes/smartcabinet-wall-support-cam-table-reference.md) - how to add hardware to SmartCabinet's Wall Support hardware library; full column reference from the software's own help text; two worked examples that exposed an unresolved X-axis sign-convention discrepancy (Task T017)
- [Process: TpaCAD tool-type auto-resolution ambiguity ("Tool for this working not found")](Processes/tpacad-tool-type-optimizer-ambiguity.md) - a real shop-floor fault on the Vitap K2-2.0 (`FA2304`): Blind bore drill operations fail to optimize when multiple spindles share the same diameter+type with no explicit Tool ID set; workaround found, permanent fix identified but not yet tried (Task T016)
