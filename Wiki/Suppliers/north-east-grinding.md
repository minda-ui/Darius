# North East Grinding Ltd — saw blade sharpening and TCT blades

**What they do for us:** sharpen the workshop's **TCT saw blades** (and a **PCD borer**), and supply new
**Stehle TCT** blades. Blades are **dropped off and collected**; each job ends with an email, *"now ready to collect"*,
with a **cash-sale receipt (.xlsx)** attached. Contact in the emails: **Darren Gardner**, 0191 257 2342. Receipts give **Unit 1, The Old Maltings,
Tanner Bank, North Shields NE30 1JH**, web `northeastgrinding.co.uk`, and a second address
`salesnortheastgrinding@outlook.com` (*not* approved for mail; nothing has come from it). Mail comes
from **`northeastgrinding@outlook.com`**, the one address approved in §6b. *Payment details in the emails are not
copied into this KB.*

**What this shows.**
- **A sharpening batch roughly every three months** (mid-January, end of April, late July), about **£200** each —
  **about £800 a year** at that rate, *for all the TCT blades sent, not only the F45's*.
- **One blade, once, was £17.88.** The batches of ~£200 are therefore **several blades each**, but how many, and
  which machine they came from, is **only in the receipts**.
- **The VAT basis varies by email**: the January correction shows a "total due" can be ex VAT.
