# North East Grinding Ltd — saw blade sharpening and TCT blades

**What they do for us:** sharpen the workshop's **TCT saw blades** (and at least one **PCD borer**), and supply new
**Stehle TCT** blades. Blades are **dropped off and collected**; each job ends with an email, *"now ready to collect"*,
with a **cash-sale receipt (.xlsx)** attached. Contact in the emails: **Darren Gardner**, 0191 257 2342. Mail comes
from **`northeastgrinding@outlook.com`**, the one address approved in §6b. *Payment details in the emails are not
copied into this KB.*

## Jobs so far, from the email bodies

| Email date | What | Total in the email | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-12 | **PCD borer** ready; saw blades dropped off the same day | **£210.06** | Corrected the same afternoon to **£252.07 = £210.06 + £42.01 VAT** — so the first figure was **ex VAT** |
| 2026-01-14 | Sharpened blades + **three new Stehle TCT saw blades** ready | **£318.60** (invoice for the new blades) | Ordered by the owner on 2026-01-12 as **Stehle TCT 300 mm × 96T × 30 mm bore**; £318.60 / 3 = **£106.20 a blade**, VAT basis not stated |
| 2026-01-19 | One TCT saw blade ready | **£17.88** | One blade — **the nearest thing to a single-sharpening price**, VAT basis not stated. Credit-account application form attached |
| 2026-04-30 | TCT saw blades ready | **£197.04** | number of blades not in the email |
| 2026-07-21 | TCT saw blades ready | **£201.24** | number of blades not in the email |

**What this shows.**
- **A sharpening batch roughly every three months** (mid-January, end of April, late July), about **£200** each —
  **about £800 a year** at that rate, *for all the TCT blades sent, not only the F45's*.
- **One blade, once, was £17.88.** The batches of ~£200 are therefore **several blades each**, but how many, and
  which machine they came from, is **only in the receipts**.
- **The VAT basis varies by email**: the January correction shows a "total due" can be ex VAT.

**Not yet known, and in the attached receipts:** line items (blades per batch, price per blade, size), and VAT on each.
*The receipts were not opened: invoices and receipts are left in mail unless the owner asks.*
