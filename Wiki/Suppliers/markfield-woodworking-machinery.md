# Markfield Woodworking Machinery Ltd (supplier)

UK dealer who sold the workshop's `FA2402` (AES SAF 10,000 STK centralised dust extractor) in
October 2024. Trades as **MWM** / mwmachinery.co.uk.

**The first supplier in this KB evidenced by a purchase rather than a manual.** The only other
Suppliers entry, [Altendorf GmbH](altendorf-gmbh.md), was created from a manufacturer's spare-parts
ordering page — it identifies who *made* `FA2303`, not who *sold* it. For `FA2301`, `FA2303` and
`FA2304` the seller has never been identified at all: invoices 100153/100154/100155 name the
customer and the goods but carry no seller letterhead. Markfield is the first supplier this KB can
name from a document, with an address, a VAT number and terms of sale.

## Terms of sale — the part worth remembering

These clauses shaped what the group had to do itself, and are quoted verbatim in the
[machine's own article](../Machinery/aes-saf-10000-stk-extractor.md):

- **Supply excludes** electrical cabling, extraction hose and blades.
- **No on-site electrical connection**: *"You are responsible for the electrical connection of your
  machinery, we do not electrically connect machinery on-site."*
- **Offloading is the customer's**: the quotation notes the customer has a forklift.
- **Payment**: 20% deposit, 80% balance prior to delivery.
- **Retention of title** until paid in full — satisfied here, the invoice is marked paid.

**Practical read:** MWM sell and deliver machines. They do not install, connect or commission them.
Anyone buying from them again should budget separately for ducting, electrical work and
commissioning, and should expect no vendor record of any of it afterwards.

## Relationship status

A **single transaction in 2024**, fully paid, with no evidence of any continuing relationship: no
service contract, no maintenance visits, no warranty correspondence, no spare-parts orders on file.
They remain the obvious first call for AES consumables and for the operating manual the KB does not
have.

**Nothing has been sent to them, and nothing should be without the owner** — committing either
company to a purchase or replying to a supplier is outside what this KB does unattended
(`CLAUDE.md` §6a).

## Open questions

- **Can they supply the AES operating manual?** The single most useful thing they could provide.
  Without it `FA2402` cannot join the Maintenance Schedule or the Fault Log. They are the seller of
  record, so they are the right first ask — for the owner to make, not this KB.
- **Consumables pricing and lead time** for the 64 filters (Ø160 × 940 mm) and the three waste
  buckets — unknown, and worth knowing before a filter set is actually needed.
- **Is there a warranty, and did it ever run?** No warranty terms were captured from either document.
- **Did they also supply anything else?** Only one invoice is in `Raw/`. Whether MWM sold the group
  other equipment before or since has not been checked.
- **Separately, and not to be conflated with Markfield:** the Vitap `FA2304` wears an **R&J
  Machinery** dealer sticker (`01455`, Hinckley), which is the first hint of who sold the 2023
  machines. That is `[confirm]` — a sticker on a machine is not a document — and it is a *different*
  company from Markfield. Do not merge the two leads.
