# Altendorf GmbH (manufacturer)

Manufacturer of the workshop's `FA2303` (Altendorf F45 ElmoDrive sliding table saw), and parent
group of Maschinenbau Hebrock GmbH, manufacturer of `FA2301`. This article covers manufacturer
contact details only — the actual UK supplier/reseller who issued invoice 100153 is a separate,
still-unidentified party (see the Machinery article's Open questions, Task T009).
