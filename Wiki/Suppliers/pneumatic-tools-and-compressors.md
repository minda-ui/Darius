# Pneumatic Tools & Compressors Ltd (supplier)

Nottingham compressed-air specialist who sold the workshop's `FA2306` — the **ABAC GENESIS rotary
screw air compressor** that feeds the pneumatics of `FA2301`, `FA2303` and `FA2304` — in November
2023.

**The second supplier in this KB evidenced by a purchase rather than a manual**, after
[Markfield Woodworking Machinery Ltd](markfield-woodworking-machinery.md). That matters more than it
sounds: for `FA2301`, `FA2303` and `FA2304` — the three production machines — the seller is *still*
unidentified, because invoices 100153/100154/100155 name the customer and the goods but carry no
seller letterhead. Two of the workshop's seven registered machines have a named, documented supplier.
The three that do the work do not.

Customer as printed: **Fishbone Drylining Ltd**, Unit 31 Point Pleasant Industrial Estate, Wallsend
NE28 6HA — the same billing entity as every other machine on the register (Task T004).

**The document's delivery line is not a delivery record.** It prints *"delivery as per inv address"*
against that Unit 31 address, and this article first repeated it as fact. The owner corrected it on
2026-09-18: **the machine was delivered to Unit 32**, where the machines it feeds already were. Unit 31
is this supplier's billing and correspondence address for the customer. **Cite this document for the
order, the price and the specification — not for where the machine went.**

### It is a proforma, and that is not a quibble

The document states twice that it is not a receipt — *"PROFORMA INVOICE. THIS IS NOT A VAT RECEIPT"*
and *"PROFORMA INVOICE IS VALID FOR 30 DAYS"*. It therefore evidences **the order**, not payment and
not delivery. It was accepted as sufficient to date the acquisition to 2023 because its own validity
window closes on 14/12/2023 and the rest of the fit-out was invoiced five days earlier — but **the VAT
invoice has never been seen** and is still wanted. See the
[machine's own article](../Machinery/abac-genesis-screw-air-compressor.md) for the full reasoning.

## Why this supplier matters now

`FA2306`'s manufacturer service label carries a printed log table — *Official Specialist / Latest
Service / Running Hours / Type of Service* — and **it is completely blank**. The machine was delivered
in November 2023, so if that label is the only record, nothing has been logged in nearly three years,
against ABAC's own stated interval of **2,000 hours or 1 year, whichever comes first**.

This company is the obvious first place to ask, on two counts: they sold the machine, and compressed
air is their trade rather than a sideline. Task **T020** carries the question.

> **Governance note.** Asking whether a machine was serviced is research. **Booking the service is
> not** — it commits the company to a payment, which is the owner's action and never an unattended one
> (`CLAUDE.md` §6a). Darius can draft the enquiry; it does not send it and it does not place the
> order.

They are also a sensible route for the **air receiver** question in Task **T021** — whether the
installation includes a receiver of the size the model designation implies, and whether a written
scheme of examination exists for it.

## What was deliberately not recorded

The proforma carries **bank details (sort code and account number)** for payment by transfer, and a
**personal mobile number**. Neither has been copied into this article, the Machinery Register or the
Document Register, per `CLAUDE.md` §6a. Noted so the omission reads as a decision, not an oversight —
the same treatment given to the banking details on the MWM quotation.

## Open questions

- **The VAT invoice** for proforma 208027 — does one exist, and what date does it carry?
- **Has this supplier ever serviced the machine** since selling it? (T020)
- **Do they hold the machine's operating manual?** `FA2306` has none, which is what keeps it out of
  the Maintenance Schedule and Fault Log.
- **Was a receiver supplied as part of the package**, and was any pressure-system paperwork issued with
  it? (T021)
