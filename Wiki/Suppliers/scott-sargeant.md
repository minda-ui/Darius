# Scott+Sargeant — the workshop's tooling supplier

**Owner, 2026-10-04:** *"https://www.scosarg.com/ is a place where I am buying tooling for workshop machinery."*

**Scott+Sargeant Woodworking Machinery Ltd** ("Scosarg"), 1 Blatchford Rd, Horsham, West Sussex RH13 5QR. A family
business since the 1930s. Sells new and used woodworking machinery (iTECH, SCM and others), **tooling** (saw blades
including **CMT**, CNC router cutters, spindle moulder tooling, planer blades, bandsaw blades) and spares. *All from
their search listings.*

## Access

**`www.scosarg.com` is behind Cloudflare bot protection**: an HTTP 403 *"Just a moment…"* challenge to automated
readers. The bare domain is refused by this environment's proxy. **The protection is not worked around**, so
**only web-search listings are readable**: product names, URLs and snippets. Prices and full specs come from the
owner, from an invoice, or from the maker's own site.

## Tooling seen so far

- **CMT 237 Xtreme PCD saw blade, Ø350 × bore 30, Z72, kerf 3.2**: "d-350-d-30-z-72-b-3-2" in the product address.
  **PCD (diamond)**, for laminates, chipboard, MDF and plastic-laminated boards.
