# AES Group (manufacturer)

Turkish manufacturer of industrial dust collection plant, and the maker of the workshop's `FA2402`
(AES SAF 10,000 STK centralised fine dust extractor). Sold into the UK through dealers — ours came
via [Markfield Woodworking Machinery Ltd](markfield-woodworking-machinery.md).

**This is a manufacturer entry, not a supplier one** — the same distinction as
[Altendorf GmbH](altendorf-gmbh.md). AES built the machine; Markfield sold it. Warranty and
technical questions point here; commercial ones point at Markfield.

## What their manual specifies for the S series

Recorded because it is the only manufacturer-authored operating information the KB holds for any AES
machine — but see the caveat below before applying any of it to `FA2402`.

**Operating environment:** −25 to +55 °C; relative humidity below 95 %; maximum 1,000 m above sea
level. Transport and storage up to +70 °C for no more than 24 hours.

**Electrical:** 380–400 V, 50 Hz, tolerance not exceeding ±10 %; earthed three-phase supply to
DIN VDE 0100; power line cable H05 RN-F, minimum 3 × 2.5 + 1.5 mm²; minimum G16 fuse. The
manufacturer disclaims responsibility for faults caused by running outside that voltage band.

**Daily checks before use:** supply cable and plug for damage or weakened insulation; moving parts
for breakage or obstruction; the on/off switch; **whether the waste bag is full**; **filter loading
— clean the filters by shaking if loaded**.

**Periodic checks:** moving and rotating parts; cable insulation for abrasion or tearing; supply
plug and switch; the **motor junction-box screws**; and **all housing and connection bolts**,
tightened if loose. The manual gives **no calendar or hours interval** for any of it — only "daily"
and "periodic".

**Fault table:** eleven symptom/cause/remedy rows — won't start; won't start despite switch on; poor
suction (clogged inlet or pipes, full bag); motor noisy; motor overheating and thermal trip; supply
cable heating and fuse tripping; motor runs continuously; fuses tripping; **electric shock on
touching the housing** (grounding circuit rusted, broken or loose). Most remedies are "notify the
service"; the user-actionable ones are unclog the inlet and pipes, empty the bag, tighten the motor
terminal, and check the earth.

**Safety posture worth noting:** fire precautions are called for explicitly, with **ABC or BC
extinguishers** recommended near the unit. That is consistent with the quotation's Part Holder
guard, which exists to stop foreign objects reaching the propeller partly as a fire measure.

## The caveat — do not apply this to `FA2402` yet

The manual covers **mobile, plug-connected, bag-collecting** units. `FA2402` is a **fixed,
centralised, hard-wired, star-delta, 720 kg** machine with **three metal waste buckets**, and its
model is **`STK`**, not `S-`. An `S-10000` is listed on the manual's cover but **its row was not
visible in the extracted technical table**, which stops at S-6500. The evidence is weighed in full
in [the machine's own article](../Machinery/aes-saf-10000-stk-extractor.md).

## Open questions

- **Is `STK 10000` the same machine as `S-10000`, or a different product line?** The single question
  that decides whether the S-series manual applies. Ask AES Europe, or Markfield.
- **A manual confirmed for `STK 10000`** — with filter-change criteria, the star-delta starting
  procedure and hours-based servicing, none of which the S-series manual carries.
- **Filter part number and specification** for the 64 Ø160 × 940 mm sleeves, and a UK source.
- **Is there an AES-authorised service agent in the UK?** The manual repeatedly says "notify the
  service" without naming one, and warranty is void on non-authorised intervention.
- **No contact has been made.** Approaching a manufacturer is the owner's to make, not this KB's
  (`CLAUDE.md` §6a).
